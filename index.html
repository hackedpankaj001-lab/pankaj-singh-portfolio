Before the cloud_

No internet in my town. So I started where the screen was.

roots
Home: Sarlahi. Came to Kathmandu for studies — now on a scholarship at Bridge Water International College, Sinamangal, living in a hostel in Baneshwor. Science, 11th grade, weekday brain on school, evening brain on builds.
age 14 · ~2022
Trading crypto at fourteen. While other kids played games, I was reading charts, learning markets, losing and compounding — self-taught economics from YouTube and whitepapers on a slow connection. First lesson: risk management, the hard way.
the TV years
I coded on an actual TV. No monitor, no laptop lab — a television as the display, typing out programs with no internet to copy from. No Stack Overflow, no AI. Trial, error, restart. That's where the autodidact instinct was forged.
the drought
Town with no internet. I'd plan code on paper, then execute it in bursts whenever connectivity existed. Constraints became a design style — build offline-first, ship fast, waste nothing.
cybersecurity phase
Tried cybersecurity — didn't stick. What stuck: OSINT. Recon, footprinting, people-searches, archive digs. That training is exactly why I could pull a complete file on the founder of Danson Solutions before this call. The dossier on this site is my homework.
2023–now
Tried a lot of things. Crypto, scrapers, bots (WhatsApp and Instagram), video generation, faceless YouTube channels, casual video editing, APK reverse-engineering, calling systems, freelancing for local clients. Some worked, most didn't. The 40+ folders on my disk are the residue of real experiments — not coursework.
"I'm not a bootcamp graduate. I'm the guy who learned by needing the thing to work — today, at 18, I freelance for Rs 6–8k/mo and build industry-level apps at night."
